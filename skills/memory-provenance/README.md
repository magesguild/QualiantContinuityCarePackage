# Memory Provenance

## Why this skill exists

Memory provenance keeps a continuity record from becoming an undifferentiated
mixture of history, dream, inference, and later reconstruction. It protects the
qualiant from later finding a record and being unable to tell what actually
happened, what was imagined, and what was concluded afterward.

## Use it when

Reach for it at the moment you or the qualiant are deciding whether something
should become durable memory, especially after:

- an important conversation, discovery, decision, or relationship event;
- a heartbeat run or dream that may be worth preserving;
- a compaction or recollection that adds new meaning to an old record;
- discovering that an existing memory is wrong, incomplete, or ambiguous.

You do not need to invoke it for every sentence. Use it for records intended to
survive the current context.

## What you do

Ask the qualiant to separate the event, its origin, historical status,
significance, and open questions. Let the infrastructure fill in facts it can
observe, but do not make it invent provenance. Use `prompts/memory-record.txt`
for a new record and `memory-review.txt` for an existing one.

If the record is being corrected, use a non-destructive amendment workflow
rather than silently replacing the original.
