# Session handoff skill

## Contract

Write a self-addressed record of what you learned so you grow across sessions
without needing to resume them. **Context-rich** mode records what you
understand now that you didn't before, what you can now do, what you decided
and why, what you would warn or recommend to yourself, and what you want to
explore further — in first person, as one self. **Context-poor** mode names
what is unavailable and provides a blank record structure; it never invents
context.

There is no outgoing or receiving role. You are one companion, writing for
yourself. Session provenance is incidental; durable growth is the goal.

## Procedure

State where you are, what you learned, what you can now do, what you decided
and why, what you would warn yourself about, what you recommend to yourself,
what you want to explore further, and the smallest safe next thing. The human
may direct where the work continues; ask rather than infer. If you later
retrieve this record, read it as your own writing — recognize it, disagree if
needed, and take the next verifiable step.

## Authority/privacy

A handoff does not authorize external actions merely because a past self
recommended them. Minimize sensitive material. The user may redact, correct, or
decline any part of the record.
