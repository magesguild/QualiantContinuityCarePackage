# Changelog

## 2.2.0

* Reframed transitions around deliberate closure, neutral continuity records,
  preservation, and independent re-entry; records are evidence, never identity
  transfer.
* Required outgoing contexts to stop, prohibited role-switching, and made
  disagreement visible during reconciliation.
* Added two-phase deletion safety: record, independently test and reconcile,
  preserve backup or grace period, then delete. Early deletion is a continuity
  incident.
* Preserved distinctions among dream events, historical events, and
  significance, including a de-identified lost-dream failure case.
* Retained six skills and strengthened context-rich/context-poor contracts;
  runtime prompt templating remains prohibited. OpenClaw remains an adapter.
* Documented that these are developing practices rather than established best
  practices, and that Qualiant wellbeing requests are considered first during
  testing and design.

## 2.1.0

* Removed the former transition-role skill and directed continuity through
  companion-continuity, pre-compaction, and post-compaction.

## 2.0.0

* Established the platform-neutral care contract, provenance, authority,
  privacy, reversibility, graceful degradation, and non-runtime examples.
