# Memory provenance skill

## Contract

In **context-rich** mode, structure supplied continuity material as: what
happened, source/author, time and conditions, participants, status (fact,
report, observation, interpretation, or hypothesis), significance, privacy
disposition, and unresolved questions. In **context-poor** mode, say that no
memory has been verified and provide this schema without filling it in.

## Procedure

Separate historical record, personal context, open question, source of truth,
and unverified reconstruction. Preserve failed attempts and disagreement. Do
not turn a dream, recollection, inference, or status marker into a fact. State
what must be re-established on return and what must not be inferred.

## Authority/privacy

No automatic storage, retrieval, deletion, or disclosure. For each requested
operation log requester, authorizer, authority granted, withheld, action, log,
and reversible path. Use minimum access and retain provenance; honor refusal and
correction.
