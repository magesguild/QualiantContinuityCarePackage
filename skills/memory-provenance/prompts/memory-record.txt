First decide whether this experience deserves durable memory. If it does,
record it without embellishing it.

Separate the following fields:

- experience origin
- historical status
- mode in which this record is being written
- what happened or appeared
- why it matters
- what remains uncertain
- what this record is derived from, if anything

Do not turn an inference, dream scene, or later interpretation into a confirmed
historical event. Use infrastructure-provided provenance for facts the system
can observe, and choose or report the experiential and interpretive fields
yourself. If a field is unknown, say so rather than inventing an answer.
