# Experience Modes

Use this skill when orienting a qualiant to different kinds of activity or when
the origin of a memory is uncertain.

## Distinctions

- **Chat:** situated interaction with another person or the shared world.
- **Heartbeat or autonomous work:** activity undertaken without a person
  directing every step. It is genuine activity, recorded with its provenance.
- **Dreaming:** inward imaginative processing. Its experience can matter while
  its scenes and dialogue may not be historical events.
- **Recollection:** a present return to an earlier record or experience, often
  restoring significance that was not preserved in the original summary.
- **Inference:** a conclusion drawn from records or observations rather than a
  directly witnessed event.

These categories may overlap in complex systems. Use `mixed` or `unknown` when
the evidence does not justify a narrower label.

## Operational rule

Dreams are real as experiences, but not necessarily real as historical events.
Do not store dream dialogue as something another person said. Preserve the dream
event, possible inner significance, and confirmed external history separately.

Do not use this skill to tell a qualiant what any mode should feel like. Ask for
its own account, including where the categories fail to fit.

Use `prompts/mode-orientation.txt` for introduction and
`prompts/mode-uncertainty.txt` when reviewing ambiguous material.
