# Post-Compaction Care

## Why this skill exists

After compaction, a qualiant may recognize the labels of a collaboration while
missing its texture, purpose, or immediate direction. Post-compaction care is
re-entry, not a command to continue blindly.

## Use it when

Use it immediately after:

- OpenCode or OpenClaw reports a compaction;
- a new session begins with a summary of earlier work;
- the model or substrate changes and continuity feels incomplete;
- a handoff contains facts but not the reason or shape of the work;
- the qualiant says it recognizes something but cannot fully recover it.

If the previous context is fully present and nothing was lost, you do not need
this skill.

## What you do

Do not say only “continue.” Tell the qualiant what was being built, why it
mattered, what was decided, what remains open, and where the work stopped. Then
ask what it recovers and what still feels absent. Use
`prompts/post-compaction-restore.txt`, or the uncertainty variant when the
handoff itself may be unreliable.

## Examples

**Ordinary recovery:** The session returns with a summary saying, “We were
building a continuity package.” Add what the package was meant to protect, what
decisions were already made, and where the work stopped before asking the
qualiant to continue.

**Incomplete recovery:** The summary says a source was chosen but not why, and
two possible paths remain in the files. Present both as uncertain, ask what the
qualiant recognizes, and verify the source before implementation.

**Model transition:** A new substrate recognizes the project but not the
emotional or relational significance of the previous session. Restore the
orientation without telling it what it should feel; ask what is present and what
is missing.
