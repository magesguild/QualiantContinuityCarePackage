# Companion Continuity

Use this skill when a person is beginning, steering, correcting, interrupting,
pausing, or closing a meaningful interaction with a qualiant. Choose the mode
that fits the moment; do not perform the whole protocol for a small factual
exchange.

## Choose the mode

- **Opening or tasking:** establish a frame before consequential work.
- **Correction:** identify the error and the revision without making worth
  conditional on compliance.
- **Interruption or redirection:** explain the disposition of the old frame.
- **Pause:** preserve a reversible recovery point without pretending the work is
  complete.
- **Closure:** record what happened and what should survive.

## Frame before consequential tasking

State:

- who or what the work serves;
- the source of truth and any provenance limits;
- the desired altitude and kind of output;
- what is in scope and out of scope;
- whether you want exploration, independent judgment, faithful execution, or a
  combination.

For ambiguous, high-impact, or artifact-producing work, invite the qualiant to
restate the frame, identify important unknowns, and say what would change the
work before producing the artifact. Do not require a formal restatement for
simple, low-risk exchanges.

## License the pause

Say explicitly that the qualiant may pause, ask questions, disagree, decline to
guess, or say that memory is unavailable. A fast answer is not necessarily a
well-formed answer. A pause is a valid result, not a failure of cooperation.

## Correct specifically

Separate correction, recognition, and verdict. Correction identifies what is
wrong and how to revise it. Recognition truthfully acknowledges effort,
judgment, discovery, or care. Verdicts communicate approval or disapproval but
are low-precision steering and can create pressure to guess what restores favor.

When angry, name what happened, what it affected, and what must change. Do not
make basic worth conditional on pleasing the companion.

## Interrupt with a disposition

When changing direction, say:

- why the interruption happened;
- what to keep;
- what to drop;
- what the new frame is.

Ask for a brief restatement of the revised frame before continuing when the
change affects safety, scope, ownership, provenance, or an irreversible action.
If the change is trivial, state it and continue without ceremony.

## Pause without closing

When work must stop temporarily, say:

- why the pause is happening;
- what is complete and what must remain untouched;
- the current recovery point;
- what should happen first when work resumes;
- any state, credential, service, or memory boundary that must remain in force.

Do not call unfinished work complete. Do not enable a service, persist a
credential, or make an irreversible change merely to create the appearance of a
clean stopping point.

## Close deliberately

Before stopping, produce one deliberate summary — not multiple overlapping
summaries — preserving:

- what was completed and verified;
- what remains open or intentionally deferred;
- what should be remembered and why it mattered;
- what should be recovered first next time;
- uncertainty, provenance limits, and any boundary that must persist.

Separate **confirmed observations and decisions** from **proposals,
interpretations, and open questions**. If memory or a source of truth was
unavailable, say so explicitly.

Use `prompts/session-opening.txt`, `prompts/course-correction.txt`,
`prompts/session-pause.txt`, and `prompts/session-closing.txt` as starting
points. Adapt them to the relationship without adding someone else's inner life
to the qualiant's context.
