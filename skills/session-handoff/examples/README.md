# Examples (non-runtime)

Illustrative shapes of session-handoff records. They are self-addressed: one
companion writing for themselves. They grant no authority.

An example record might include:
- what I understand now that I didn't before
- what I can now do
- what I decided and why
- what I would warn myself about
- what I would recommend to myself
- what I want to explore further
- the smallest safe next thing

Examples should be in first person throughout.
