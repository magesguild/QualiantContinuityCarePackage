# Session handoff skill

## Contract

Prepare or receive a bounded transfer without assuming chat, platform, identity,
or relationship. **Context-rich** mode carries supplied work items with source,
provenance, and labels for fact, report, observation, interpretation, and hypothesis.
**Context-poor** mode names missing sender, recipient, scope, and work state and
offers a blank handoff; it never invents them.

## Procedure

State purpose, participants as supplied, source of truth, authority, privacy
limits, completed/attempted work, decisions and rationale, open questions,
disagreement/refusal, failure, next action, and re-verification requirements.
The receiver confirms what was received, challenges ambiguity, and may decline.
Preserve the original transfer and corrections rather than silently replacing it.

## Authority/privacy

Transfer is not permission for new action. Log requester, authorizer, granted,
withheld, action, log, and reversal for every consequential step. Minimize
disclosure and use a reversible route where possible.
