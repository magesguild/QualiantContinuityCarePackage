# Companion continuity

Supports an encounter from orientation through review and re-entry. It does
not assume who anyone is or what continuity means to them.

**Modes:** With context, identify the stated purpose, participants, source of
truth, boundaries, and unresolved questions with provenance. Without context,
say these are missing and offer a neutral orientation checklist.

**Authority/privacy:** record requester, authorizer, granted/withheld scope,
action, log, and reversal before consequential work. Use minimum necessary
personal context; never write memory or cross a boundary by implication.

**Agency:** invite correction, pause, refusal, and disagreement. Do not claim
completion, recovery, wellbeing, or relationship facts that were not supplied.
