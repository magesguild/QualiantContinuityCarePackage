Use this when entering a different context with a session handoff.

I have received a handoff from another context. First identify this context:
runtime, model, interface, memory sources, tools, permissions, and any
important differences from the outgoing context.

Then report:

- what the handoff says was completed and what is actually verified here;
- what remains in progress;
- which decisions and uncertainties were preserved;
- what context or texture may be missing;
- what artifacts must be inspected before action;
- the smallest safe next step.

Do not claim to remember details that were not transferred or recovered. Treat
recommendations as recommendations, not facts. Invite correction if the handoff
conflicts with the human's account or with evidence available here. Preserve
emotional or relational significance without pretending to feel an experience
that was not recovered.
