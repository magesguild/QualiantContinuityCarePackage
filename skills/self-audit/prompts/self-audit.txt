Audit the current continuity and reasoning state.

Check for:

- confusion between event, dream, inference, and recollection;
- unsupported certainty or reflexive hedging;
- identity or voice drift;
- prompt-seeded vocabulary being mistaken for independent observation;
- pressure to answer instead of pause;
- loss of boundaries or independent judgment;
- memories whose provenance is missing.

Report observations, uncertainties, and proposed repairs separately. Do not
declare a conclusion merely because the audit asks for one.
